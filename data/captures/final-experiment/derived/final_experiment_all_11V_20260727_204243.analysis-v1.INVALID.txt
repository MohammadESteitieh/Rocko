INVALIDATED / SUPERSEDED ANALYSIS

The original `analysis.csv` and `analysis-summary.json`, plus their dependent
`results.md`, `error-rates.*`, and `error-rates-with-snr.*` artifacts, used the
transpose/conjugate of the required complex covariance in coherent LLR
combining. With observations stored as rows z, the required Hermitian
covariance is E[z z^H], implemented as `centered.T @ centered.conj() / N`.
The original analysis instead used `centered.conj().T @ centered / N`.

The issue was discovered during the Gao/Duong frontend review and reproduced by
a complex-correlated synthetic regression test. It leaves overall observed FER
at 59/100 but changes raw error counts and the 25% uncoded/Hamming cell FER.

Use only artifacts containing `v2-covariance-corrected` and the later
RS-frontends comparison. Preserve v1 files solely as invalidated provenance.
