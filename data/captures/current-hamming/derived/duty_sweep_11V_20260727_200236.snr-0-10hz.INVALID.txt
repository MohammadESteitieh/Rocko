INVALIDATED ANALYSIS

The files `duty_sweep_11V_20260727_200236.snr-0-10hz.csv` and
`duty_sweep_11V_20260727_200236.snr-0-10hz.md` treated all active-minus-off
power from zero to 10 Hz as signal. That definition incorrectly classified
out-of-band active-window drift or movement as signal and produced the
misleading positive commanded-1% value.

Use `duty_sweep_11V_20260727_200236.snr-carrier-vs-0-10hz-noise.csv` instead.
The corrected signal numerator contains only power-subtracted carrier-band
power; the denominator contains all transmitter-off noise from zero to 10 Hz.
