# Carrier SNR against zero-to-10 Hz noise

Run: `duty_sweep_11V_20260727_200236`  
Setup: 11 V, 3 m, two coded bits/s, receiver pin restored.

| Commanded duty | Carrier SNR against 0-10 Hz noise | Carrier SNR against 7.25-8.75 Hz noise |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100% | -14.42 dB | 12.72 dB |
| 50% | -15.75 dB | 8.78 dB |
| 25% | -20.91 dB | 3.51 dB |
| 10% | -30.09 dB | -6.31 dB |
| 1% | -27.66 dB | -3.87 dB |

The signal numerator is only the power-subtracted 7.25-8.75 Hz carrier power.
For the wide-noise result, the denominator is all transmitter-off power from
zero to 10 Hz, including in-band noise. Therefore the wide-noise SNR cannot
exceed the in-band SNR.

The superseded `snr-0-10hz` table incorrectly treated any excess active-window
power anywhere from zero to 10 Hz as signal. Low-frequency movement and drift
could therefore inflate its result, most visibly at commanded 1%. Preserve that
table only as an invalidated analysis artifact; do not cite it.

The 10%/1% ordering remains nonmonotonic because both carrier estimates are near
the noise floor and their transmitter-off wide-band noise powers differed. Do
not force monotonicity or replace either value with a duty-derived estimate.
