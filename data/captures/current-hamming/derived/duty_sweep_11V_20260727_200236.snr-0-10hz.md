The zero-to-10 Hz values use a fourth-order, zero-phase 10 Hz low-pass after
subtracting one frozen transmitter-off baseline per sensor. Active power is
compared with the corresponding 10-second transmitter-off window, and sensor
powers are pooled before calculating SNR. Frame boundaries are the frozen
manifest-timestamp boundaries from the regenerated matrix.

This wide band includes DC drift, movement, and other low-frequency power. Its
nonmonotonic duty ordering—especially the positive 1% result—is evidence that
wide-band nuisance power dominates some frames, not evidence that commanded 1%
produced a stronger 8 Hz carrier. Use 7.25-8.75 Hz as the primary carrier SNR
and retain zero-to-10 Hz as a separately labeled diagnostic.
